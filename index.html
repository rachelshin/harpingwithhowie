about the show

Real stories, told by peers

An after‑school special for adults. Participants in the award‑winning Howie the Harp Specialist Training program in New York City share their lived experience, strength, and hope. What worked, what didn't, and what they learned along the way.

Our hosts bring wisdom and levity to the things that couldn't break us.

What is peer support?

One of the best‑kept secrets in mental healthcare. It's when people with lived experience in mental health support other people who are struggling or in crisis.

Studies show that peer support is as effective as, and in some cases more effective than, traditional mental health treatments.
